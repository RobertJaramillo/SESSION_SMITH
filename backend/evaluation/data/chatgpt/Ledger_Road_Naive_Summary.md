### Campaign Summary: "The Auditors of Veyr"

**Setting:**
The campaign unfolds in the damp river kingdom of Veyr, particularly focusing on the towns of Brackenford and Ilyr’s Gate. The region is steeped in history, with hidden dangers and old imperial roads. The central conflict arises from the old debts and records tied to the imperial road system, which are being manipulated by various factions.

**Main Plot:**
The party, initially hired to escort a merchant's ledger, finds themselves embroiled in a complex web of historical injustices, missing people, and bureaucratic corruption. Their journey reveals the existence of the Sable Clerk, a mysterious entity tied to the road's accounting system, and the implications of historical records that have been altered or hidden.

### Key Sessions Overview:

- **Session 0-1:** The party is introduced in Brackenford, where they accept a job to transport a merchant ledger, only to discover the previous couriers have gone missing. Suspicion arises as they explore the ledger's seals and venture into the wilderness, encountering bandits and an abandoned camp.

- **Session 2-3:** The investigation leads to a mysterious symbol, "Blue Aster," and the discovery of an old milestone. They encounter Salla, a fake tax collector, who reveals hints about missing ledger pages and the old road's secrets.

- **Session 4-6:** The party travels to Ilyr's Gate, delving deeper into the mystery of the missing couriers and the ledger. They meet various NPCs, including Lysa Ardwine, and uncover connections to old military orders and the Sable Year disaster.

- **Session 7-10:** The party's mistrust of authority grows as they confront the realities of the past, including the missing Mara Renn and the implications of the road's magic. They deal with bandits, ghosts, and the complexities of the road's contracts.

- **Session 11-15:** Tensions rise as they confront the Aster Table and the implications of the road's contracts. The party’s actions begin to have real consequences, leading to the revelation of hidden truths about the Sable Clerk.

- **Session 16-20:** After uncovering the truth behind the Sable Year, the party navigates political intrigue and public scrutiny. They work to protect witnesses and gather evidence while contending with the machinations of Lady Cairn and other factions.

- **Session 21-30:** The party’s actions culminate in a public audit, where they confront the consequences of their journey, including the fate of the Sable Clerk and the under-road's authority. They must navigate the legalities and moral implications of their findings.

- **Session 31-40:** The campaign's final chapters see the party return to Brackenford, deal with the fallout of their actions, and confront powerful adversaries like Auditor-Marshal Caul. They work to secure justice for the victims of the Sable Year while managing their newfound responsibilities as auditors of the road.

- **Session 41-50:** The campaign concludes with a public audit that reveals the truths of the past while ensuring the safety of the present. The party establishes a new charter board to oversee the under-road, marking a significant shift in the political landscape of Veyr.

### Key Themes and Elements:
- **Corruption and Accountability:** The campaign explores themes of bureaucratic corruption, the manipulation of records, and the importance of truth in governance.
  
- **Historical Injustice:** The Sable Year disaster serves as a backdrop, highlighting the long-lasting effects of historical decisions on the present.

- **Moral Ambiguity:** The players must navigate complex moral choices, balancing personal interests with the greater good.

- **Community and Relationships:** Throughout their journey, the party builds relationships with NPCs and witnesses, emphasizing the importance of community in confronting systemic issues.

### Conclusion:
The campaign ends with the party successfully establishing a new system of accountability in Veyr, allowing for a brighter future while acknowledging the complexities and challenges that remain. The characters have grown and changed, ready to face whatever comes next in their lives, be it as auditors, heroes, or something else entirely.
