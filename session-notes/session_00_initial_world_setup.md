**Category tags:** `timeline`, `post_cataclysm`, `official_history`, `concord_era`

#### era_s0_001

The game begins about 90 years after a major flood destroyed the old capital. People call it the Flood of Bells because the bells of the drowned city supposedly rang for three days underwater. The current government teaches that the flood was divine punishment, but that may not be true.

**Summary:** The campaign begins roughly 90 years after the Flood of Bells, a disaster officially described as divine punishment but possibly hiding a deeper truth.

### Politics

**Category tags:** `government`, `corruption`, `nobles`, `commoners`, `public_unrest`

#### politics_s0_001

The main political power should be the Iron Court. They claim to keep order after the flood, but they use old laws, debt, tariffs, and oath contracts to control commoners. Nobles can usually buy their way around the rules.

**Summary:** The Iron Court controls Kestrel Vale through law, debt, tariffs, and oath contracts, while nobles enjoy exceptions that commoners cannot afford.

### Laws

**Category tags:** `oaths`, `contracts`, `punishments`, `tariffs`, `legal_corruption`

#### laws_s0_001

Law in this world should revolve around spoken oaths. A contract spoken in front of a witness stone becomes magically binding. The problem is that the rich can pay for exceptions and sealed clauses, while poor people get punished publicly if they break an oath.

**Summary:** Law is based on magically binding spoken oaths, but wealthy people can buy exceptions while poor people suffer public punishment.

**Entry tags:** `river_oath`, `witness_stones`, `legal_system`, `class_injustice`

### Magic Systems

**Category tags:** `oath_magic`, `leylines`, `truth`, `lies`, `witness_stones`

#### magic_systems_s0_001

Magic should not be everywhere. The most important magic is oath magic. When someone swears a true oath near a witness stone, there is a faint silver sound or glow. Lies, broken promises, or forced oaths create ash-black marks or a painful discord that some people can sense.

**Summary:** The main magic system is oath magic: true oaths create silver resonance, while lies or coerced vows create ash-black discord.
